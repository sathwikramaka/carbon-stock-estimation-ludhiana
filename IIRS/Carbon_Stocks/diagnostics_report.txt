gridded soil product, where bulk density is derived from organic carbon
by a pedotransfer function. If so, the predictors are not independent
measurements and the R2 is inflated.

  3. PERMUTATION IMPORTANCE — honest importance on held-out data
========================================================================
The dashboard chart uses impurity importance, which is biased towards
continuous features with many split points. Permutation importance
measures how much test performance actually drops when a feature is
shuffled. Large gaps between the two rankings mean the chart misleads.

BGC = SOC x BD x 30 / 10. If removing it collapses the score, the model

========================================================================
  5. SPATIAL BLOCK CV — scores when neighbours cannot leak
========================================================================
A random split puts adjacent 250 m cells in both train and test. Because
neighbouring cells are nearly identical, the model can look up an answer
it has already seen. Blocking by geography removes that shortcut and
gives the score you would get on genuinely new ground.

Above-ground (NPP)     folds: 0.2204  0.4837  0.2402  0.5259  0.5400
                       mean R2 = 0.4020  (sd 0.1416)

Below-ground (SOC)     folds: 0.9224  0.9675  0.9665  0.9405  0.9432
                       mean R2 = 0.9480  (sd 0.0171)

The spatial CV column is the number to report as honest predictive skill.

  6. BASELINE — how much better than guessing the mean?
========================================================================

a high R2 on a low-variance target may still be nearly useless.

========================================================================
  WHAT TO DO WITH THIS
========================================================================
1. Report the spatial CV scores as your headline model performance, and
   the random-split scores alongside them as the optimistic case. Stating
   both, with the reason for the gap, is stronger than quoting one number.

2. If the bd_gcm3 ablation showed a large drop, say plainly in the report
   that the below-ground R2 is inflated by shared provenance between the
   soil predictors and the SOC target, and that bulk density additionally
   appears in the carbon conversion formula.

3. If any feature came out as COARSE in section 1, it is acting as a
   regional identifier. Either drop it and re-check the score, or state
   that its apparent importance reflects spatial position, not process.

4. The district carbon total is 96% below-ground, so corrections to the
   above-ground model will barely move it. Effort belongs on the SOC side.

========================================================================
  3. BELOW-GROUND MODEL COMPARISON
========================================================================
The soil predictors correlate 0.84-0.97 with each other and with SOC,
and the signs are physically inverted (sand and clay should oppose each
other; bulk density should fall as organic carbon rises). This points to
all four layers being outputs of one gridded soil product built from a
shared covariate stack, rather than independent measurements.

model                          random R2  spatial R2      RMSE       MAE
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Original (all features)           0.9665      0.9481     1.624     1.195
Without bulk density              0.9597      0.9389     1.756     1.289
Terrain + class only              0.3021      0.2081     6.556     3.687
Coordinates only                  0.6869      0.1724     6.414     3.571

Below-ground scores hold up under spatial validation, so the model is
not simply memorising neighbours. What the score measures is agreement
with one soil product, not accuracy against field-measured carbon.
